# Usage

## 1. Add a Card

Click **+ Add Card** in the toolbar.

Choose one of the available card layouts:

- Hero Banner
- Section Label
- Page Card
- Action Card
- External Link
- Media / Gallery
- Form Card
- Data Card
- Payment Card

Enter the title and description. Depending on the selected type, additional fields such as URL or price may appear.

Choose an accent color and select **Add Card**.

## 2. Move a Card

Click and drag a card to reposition it on the canvas.

The card becomes selected while it is being moved.

## 3. Edit Card Content

Several card fields are directly editable on the canvas.

Click editable text and change its contents. Supported editable values depend on the card type and include titles, descriptions, URLs, and payment prices.

## 4. Create Connections

Open the **Connections** panel.

### Step 1 — Choose From

Use the **From** selector to choose the source card.

### Step 2 — Choose Targets

Select one or more cards under **Connect to**.

Cards that already have a connection from the selected source are shown as linked and cannot be selected again.

### Step 3 — Add a Label

Optionally enter a label such as:

```text
links to
next step
submits to
```

The label is applied to the connections being created.

### Step 4 — Choose a Color

Select a connection color.

Click **Connect Selected** to create the connections.

## 5. Edit an Existing Connection

The Connections panel lists existing connections.

For each connection you can:

- Change its label.
- Change its color.
- Remove it.

A connection can also be removed from the canvas by double-clicking its connection path.

## 6. Zoom and Pan

Use the `+` and `−` buttons in the toolbar to change zoom.

Use **Reset** to return the canvas view to its initial position and zoom.

You can also use the canvas interaction described in the toolbar hint to zoom and pan the workspace.

## 7. Delete a Card

Hover over a card to reveal its delete button.

Click the `✕` button to remove that card.

When a card is deleted, connections associated with it should also be removed by the application's deletion logic.

## 8. Clear the Board

Click **🗑 Clear** to clear the current flowchart.

Use this when you want to start a new diagram.

## Recommended Workflow

A practical workflow is:

```text
Add Card
   ↓
Arrange Cards
   ↓
Edit Card Details
   ↓
Open Connections
   ↓
Choose From Card
   ↓
Choose Target Cards
   ↓
Add Label / Color
   ↓
Connect Selected
   ↓
Review and Adjust
```

Use different card types to represent different stages of a product or website flow, then use connections to show how users or data move between those stages.
