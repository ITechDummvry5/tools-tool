[ FAQ ]

Frequently
asked questions

Quick answers about where your data lives, how to back it up and how far ahead you can plan.

Where is my data stored?

In this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear the site data for this page, your subjects go with it, so export a backup now and then.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open the page and start a subject.

How do I back up or move my data?

Use Export to download a backup file, and Import to load it back, on this device or another one. Press E or I as a shortcut.

How far ahead can I plan?

A what-if plan looks at most 15 years ahead. You can save several plans and compare them side by side.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The page makes no network requests, so once it is open it keeps working without a connection.
